Agile vs Waterfall

FeatureAgile ModelWaterfall Model
ApproachIterative and incrementalSequential and linear
FlexibilityHighly flexible; changes can be made anytimeRigid; changes are difficult once the phase is complete
PhasesContinuous development, testing, and feedbackRequirement → Design → Development → Testing → Deployment
Customer InvolvementHigh; frequent feedback and collaborationLow; feedback is mostly at the end
TestingContinuous testingAfter development
Risk HandlingEasy to adapt to changesHigh risk if requirements change later
DeliveryFrequent releasesOne final release
Best ForDynamic projectsStable projects
ExamplesSoftware products, mobile apps, startupsGovernment projects, infrastructure

Agile Manifesto

  1. Individuals and interactions > processes and tools
  2. Working software > comprehensive documentation
  3. Customer collaboration > contract negotiation
  4. Responding to change > following a plan

What is Scrum?

Scrum is an Agile framework used for software development and project management. It delivers work in small Sprints with continuous feedback.

Roles

  • Product Owner - Vision & backlog
  • Scrum Master - Coach & facilitator
  • Development Team - Builds product

Artifacts

  • Product Backlog (PO)
  • Sprint Backlog (Dev Team)
  • Increment (Dev Team)
  • Definition of Done
  • Burndown Chart
  • Velocity Chart

Scrum Ceremonies

  1. Sprint Planning
  2. Daily Stand-up
  3. Sprint Review
  4. Sprint Retrospective

Workflow

  1. Product Backlog
  2. Sprint Planning
  3. Sprint Execution
  4. Daily Stand-up
  5. Sprint Review
  6. Retrospective
  7. Next Sprint
