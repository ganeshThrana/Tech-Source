collaboration > contract negotiation 4️⃣ Responding to change > following a plan What is Scrum? Scrum is an Agile framework used for software development and project management. It helps teams deliver work in small, manageable iterations (Sprints) while continuously improving through feedback. Scrum Roles 🏅 Product Owner 🏗️ Defines the vision, prioritizes tasks in the backlog, and ensures the team builds the right product. Scrum Master 🏆 A coach and facilitator who removes roadblocks and ensures Scrum principles are followed. (Agile Certification) Development Team 👨‍💻 Builds and delivers the product within the Sprint timeline. Scrum Artifacts: Product Backlog - (Maintained by PO- product owner) Sprint Backlog - (Dev Team) Increment - (Dev Team) Supporting Artifacts: (TBD) Definition of Done (DOD) Burndown chart Velocity Chart Scrum Ceremonies: 1. Sprint Planning (Before the Sprint) 📅 🔹 Purpose: Decide what work will be done in the upcoming Sprint. 🔹 Participants: Scrum Master, Product Owner, Development Team. 🔹 Timebox: 2-4 hours (for a 2-week Sprint). 🔹 Process: The Product Owner presents the top-priority items from the Product Backlog. The team discusses, estimates, and selects tasks for the Sprint Backlog. The team sets a Sprint Goal (e.g., "Implement the login feature"). ✅ Outcome: A well-defined Sprint Backlog (list of tasks for the Sprint). 🔹 2. Daily Stand-up (Every Day of the Sprint) ⏰ 🔹 Purpose: A quick status update to track progress and identify blockers. 🔹 Participants: Development Team, Scrum Master (Product Owner is optional). 🔹 Timebox: 15 minutes. 🔹 Process: Each team member answers 3 questions: What did I do yesterday? What will I do today? Do I have any blockers? ✅ Outcome: Everyone is aligned, and blockers are addressed quickly. 🔹 3. Sprint Review (End of the Sprint) ✅ 🔹 Purpose: Show completed work to stakeholders and gather feedback. 🔹 Participants: Scrum Team, Stakeholders, Customers. 🔹 Timebox: 1-2 hours. 🔹 Process: The team demonstrates the features completed in the Sprint. Stakeholders give feedback, which may be added to the backlog. The Product Owner updates the backlog for the next Sprint. ✅ Outcome: A clear view of progress and feedback from stakeholders. 🔹 4. Sprint Retrospective (After the Sprint) 🔄 🔹 Purpose: Improve processes by reflecting on what went well and what didn’t. 🔹 Participants: Scrum Team. 🔹 Timebox: 1 hour. 🔹 Process: The team discusses: What went well? - On-time delivery What didn’t go well? - Extra efforts for Alex and Prem What can be improved? - Leave less ✅ Outcome: Actionable steps to improve the next Sprint. Scrum Workflow (Step-by-Step) 1️⃣ Product Owner creates the Product Backlog (list of features and tasks). 2️⃣ Sprint Planning → The team picks items from the backlog for the Sprint. 3️⃣ Sprint Execution → The team works on the Sprint tasks. 4️⃣ Daily Stand-up → Quick meeting to track progress and solve issues. 5️⃣ Sprint Review → The team presents completed work to stakeholders. 6️⃣ Sprint Retrospective → The team reflects on the Sprint and improves. 7️⃣ Next Sprint begins → The cycle repeats!
